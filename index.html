Why AURA exists

Built with T1D in mind.

AURA started from a simple question: “What will this meal do to my glucose?” Carb counts alone don't tell the whole story — the same 60 g of carbs can behave very differently depending on the food and the order you eat it in.

So AURA pairs every meal with a glycemic index estimate and a chrono-order hint, and keeps a private, on-device diary of how your plates look over time. You get the numbers; you and your care team make the decisions.

GI estimate with every meal Chrono-order suggestions No insulin math — ever

AURA is a wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or calculate insulin doses.

Privacy by design

Your diary never leaves your phone.

AURA works without registration and without a cloud account. Meals are stored in a local database on your device, and food photos are used only to estimate nutrition — not kept on our servers.

Questions, answered

Is AURA a medical app?+

No. AURA is a wellness tool for nutrition awareness. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or calculate insulin doses. Always consult your healthcare provider about diabetes management.

Do I need an account?+

No. The app works without registration — install, open, start scanning. Your diary lives on your phone, and nothing is tied to your identity.

What does it cost?+

The core scanner and diary are free. An optional Pro subscription with extended insights is planned for a future update — the free core stays free.

Which devices are supported?+

AURA runs on Android 7.0 and newer, on phones of any size. The app is available in English, Ukrainian, Russian, Italian and Spanish.
